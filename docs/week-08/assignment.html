Week 08 Assignment — Phase 6, the campaign delivered

DSGN 105 · Digital Imaging

Project phases, scored on the project rubric. The final image set completes component C; the walkthrough is component G, 5% of your campaign.

Submit before your walkthrough slot, not after. The term ends this week, and there is no later session.

What to submit

1 The final image set — revised, consistent after revision, five to seven images
2 The working files behind them, layers and masks live
3 Your unedited originals
4 Your specification statements from Phase 4
5 A disclosure statement — which images are yours, where anything else came from, how generative AI was used
6 A note on where else in the set you applied what the reading-order test revealed — including any image you deliberately left alone, and why

Item 6 is what 8.1 is assessed on. Items 1–5 complete work already begun.

What a complete answer looks like

Item 6, which is the part students most often skip:

The test told me viewers were going to a bright background before they got to my subject. I brought that down in the lead image, then looked at the other five.

Three had the same problem — a bright wall or window I hadn't noticed because I was looking at the person. I brought all three down by roughly the same amount, which also meant re-checking the treatment afterwards, because darkening three of six pulled the set apart. I ended up lifting the shadows very slightly across all six to bring them back together.

I left image four alone. The bright doorway behind the subject is the brightest thing in the frame and viewers do go there first — but that is what I want in that image, because the doorway is what the campaign is about. It is the only one where the brightness is doing the work rather than competing with it.

What that demonstrates: the revision went across the set rather than to one photograph, the student noticed that the revision disturbed the treatment and dealt with it, and the exception is defended rather than overlooked — which is the Exceeds descriptor.

What does not count

  • Revising only the tested image. It treats the test as being about one photograph, which is the one thing it is not about.
  • A set that no longer holds together. The revision will have pulled at your treatment. Checking that is part of the work, not an extra.
  • Missing originals. Not a lost mark — a lost outcome. Nothing else in this course assesses retouching.
  • A walkthrough covering three images well and passing over the rest. That is the Approaching descriptor for component G.
  • A walkthrough ordered by production history. Nobody has a reason to care what order you shot in.
  • "I'll send it tonight." Tonight is after the last session of the term.

Use the glossary

No new terms this week. Everything you need is already yours — and using it is part of what a walkthrough demonstrates. Most likely to come up: treatment, consistency, reading order, compositional mechanism, destination, specification statement, provenance, retention, unedited original. All in the Glossary.

Rubric

LO 3 — Composition and hierarchy

Apply compositional principles and visual hierarchy in the creation of digital imagery.

8.1 is where the Exceeds descriptor for component E is earned — applying the change beyond the tested image.

Level
Exceeds The revision went across the set, the student can say what the test revealed about their own habits, and any exception is defended
Meets The revision follows from the test result and is applied beyond the image that was tested
Approaching Only the tested image was revised
Not yet No revision, or a revision unrelated to what the viewers reported

LO 4 — Editing and retouching

Edit and retouch photographs to enhance visual clarity, consistency, and communicative impact.

Completes component C. The set is judged in its final state.

Level
Exceeds The treatment carries meaning across the set and still holds after the revision
Meets Before-and-after pairs show the edit changed what the photograph communicates, and the treatment holds across the whole set
Approaching The treatment drifted when the revision was applied and was not re-checked
Not yet The unedited originals are absent

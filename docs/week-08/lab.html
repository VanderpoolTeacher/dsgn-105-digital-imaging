This week inverts the usual arrangement. Normally you do something in the lab and submit afterwards. Here you submit first and then account for what you submitted.

What you are doing

Walking a room through your campaign, image by image, and answering questions about it — including about an image you did not plan to discuss.

The running order is published. Slots are fixed. If the people at the start run over, the people at the end lose time they did not cost, so the slots hold.

Part 1 · Before your slot

  1. Confirm your final image set is submitted — revised, consistent, with working files, originals, and specification statements.
  2. Have your set ready to display as a whole as well as image by image. It stays visible while you talk.
  3. Know which image you would least like to be asked about. You will be asked about one.

Part 2 · Your walkthrough

Go through your campaign, image by image. For each one:

  • What you intended it to do in the campaign
  • What you did to it — and your originals are submitted, so this is checkable
  • Why it is in the set at all

Structure it by your argument, not by the order you shot things. What do people currently believe, which image contradicts it, what happens next. That is your week 7 sequence, spoken.

Part 3 · Questions

You will be asked about an image you passed over quickly. Every student is.

This is stated in advance so it is not a trick. It is also the only part of the walkthrough that could not have been prepared, which is exactly why it is the part being assessed.

If the honest answer is "that one is in the set because I took it and it fits the treatment" — say that. It is a better answer than a justification invented on the spot.

Part 4 · While others present

Watch. Every walkthrough is somebody accounting for choices you also had to make, and this is the last time in the course you see that.

Before you leave

  • Everything is submitted. The term ends this week. There is no later session, no extension, and nothing outstanding gets collected afterwards.
  • Your originals are in. Without them your retouching cannot be marked at all, and this is the only place in the course it is assessed.

What you hand in

See the assignment — submitted before your slot, not after.

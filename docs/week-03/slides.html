Which of these could be printed at A3?

Pixels needed = printed size in inches × target ppi

A3 ≈ 11.7 × 16.5 in · at 300 ppi → ≈ 3510 × 4950 px

3 / 8

Resolution is not a property of the file

An image is 3000 × 2000 pixels.
It becomes 300 ppi when you decide to print it 10 inches wide.

Pixel dimensions exist in the file. Resolution is a plan for them.

4 / 8

ppi and dpi

ppi — pixels per inch. A property of your image. Yours.
dpi — dots per inch. A property of the printer. Not yours.

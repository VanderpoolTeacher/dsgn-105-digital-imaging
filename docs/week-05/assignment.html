Week 05 Assignment — The composite

DSGN 105 · Digital Imaging

Module summative · 10% of your course grade.

This is the last construction assessment before campaign component C — the image set, worth 25% of your campaign and the largest single component in it. What is graded here is graded again there.

What to submit

1 The working file — masks live and revisable, layers intact, nothing flattened
2 One export of the composite
3 Two or three sentences naming the worst point on your join, what you did about it, and where your partner marked it

What a complete answer looks like

A subject with a genuinely difficult edge — hair against a busy background — masked rather than deleted, placed on a supplied plate, with the light direction corrected so the shadow side matches.

The note reads:

My partner and I both marked the same place: the hair on the right shoulder, where the original grey wall was showing through as a pale fringe. It was invisible at fit-to-screen and obvious at 100% against the darker plate. I painted the mask back and then feathered it slightly, which fixed the fringe but softened the edge more than the left side — so I went back and matched them. The light was the second problem: my subject is lit from the left and the plate from the right, so I flipped the plate rather than relighting the subject.

What that demonstrates: the mask was revised rather than rebuilt, the inspection actually happened at 100% and independently, the fix created a second problem that was noticed and corrected, and the light match was treated as part of the join rather than as separate polish.

What does not count

  • A deleted selection. It exports to an identical picture and demonstrates nothing about 5.2, which asks for a mask that can be revised.
  • A flattened file. Nothing to assess.
  • A mask that was never changed. Revisability is the outcome. A mask that was right first time shows a lucky selection.
  • An easy subject. A hard-edged object on a plain background is isolated in ninety seconds and tests none of this.
  • A join checked only at fit-to-screen. It will be opened at 100% when it is marked. That is not a trap; it is stated here.
  • A perfect edge with mismatched light. 5.3 is a whole-image judgment. A flawless cut-out lit from the wrong side still reads as two photographs.

Use the glossary

The terms that matter this week: selection, mask, composite, join, and from Module 1, non-destructive, working file, flattened. All are in the Glossary.

Rubric

LO 1 — Raster software

Use industry-standard software to create, edit, and manipulate raster-based digital images for print and screen.

This week evidences the construction half at higher stakes than week 1. Print and screen destinations are assessed in campaign component D.

Level
Exceeds The join survives inspection at 100% on a machine other than the one it was built on, and the student can say what the fix cost elsewhere in the image
Meets The subject is isolated with an edge that survives the new background, the mask is live and was revised, and the two sources agree about light
Approaching A clean edge with mismatched light or grain — or a mask that was never revised, so revisability is unevidenced
Not yet A deleted selection or a flattened file. Nothing left to assess

The same four levels apply to every assignment in this course. Full descriptors: competency rubric.

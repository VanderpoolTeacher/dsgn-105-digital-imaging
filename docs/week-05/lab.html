Week 05 Lab — Composite something difficult

DSGN 105 · Digital Imaging

What you are doing

Compositing one hard subject onto a supplied background, cleanly enough that the join does not announce itself at full size — and revising your mask at least once on the way, because revisability is the thing being demonstrated.

You are also starting to edit your campaign images, and taking part in the second critique session.

If you did not shoot last week, take a supplied photograph set so you can do the assessment. Be clear with yourself about the difference: the lab is recoverable, and your campaign schedule is not.

Part 1 · Composite

  1. Open your hard subject — soft edges, hair, glass, motion. If you brought a bottle on a white background, go back to your own photographs; an easy edge teaches nothing.
  2. Take a supplied background plate. They are provided so this stays about isolation rather than about sourcing.
  3. Isolate the subject. Choose the tool by asking what kind of edge is this, not by habit.
  4. Use a mask. Do not delete. A deleted selection and a masked one look identical in the export — the difference is the whole point and it only exists in the file.
  5. Revise your mask at least once after you first build it. If it was right first time, make it harder: try it against a darker plate.

Part 2 · Match the light

  1. Look at the whole image, not the edge.
  2. Check the two sources agree about light direction, light quality, colour temperature, and grain and sharpness.
  3. Fix whichever disagrees most. A perfectly cut subject lit from the wrong side still reads as two photographs.

Part 3 · Check at 100%

This is what the assignment is marked on.

  1. Zoom your own composite to 100% and travel along the join.
  2. Mark the worst point.
  3. Hand your file to a partner. They do the same, on a different machine.
  4. Compare. If you marked different places, neither of you has looked properly — go again.
  5. Fix the worst point. Then check again.

Part 4 · Start editing your campaign

  1. Open your own campaign photographs.
  2. Begin editing toward the direction you chose in the direction study.
  3. Work non-destructively. These become the image set due next week.

Part 5 · Critique session 2

The second of four. On campaign work in progress. Cover clarity, ethical use, and appropriateness every time, and be specific enough that the person could act on it tomorrow.

Before you leave

  • Your mask has been revised, not just built.
  • Two people have inspected your join at 100%, on two machines, and agree where the worst point is.
  • You have opened your own campaign images and started. Next week the set is due.

What you are doing

Three things, in this order, and the order is not a suggestion.

Printing has a queue and a failure mode. Finding viewers takes time. Sequencing depends on nobody but you. If you do them in any other order you will discover a print problem on the last day.

Part 1 · The output package — do this first

  1. Confirm both destinations are named concretely — a print size and stock, a platform and placement. Not "print" and "digital".
  2. Export both from one working file.
  3. Write a specification statement for each: pixel dimensions and resolution, file format, colour system, and why, for that destination.
  4. Print the print destination. Actually print it. A specification for a print that was never printed has not been checked.
  5. Reopen both delivered files. Read the numbers off the files, not off the export dialogue. Reconcile against what you wrote.

Part 2 · The reading-order test

  1. Your intended order and exact questions should already be written. If not, write them now — before you show anybody anything.
  2. Find at least two viewers from outside this class.
  3. Ask your written questions. Add nothing. Do not say what the campaign is about until afterwards.
  4. Record what they said verbatim, including the answers you did not want.
  5. Decide your revision: which mechanism produced what they actually saw, and what you will change.
  6. Look for the same habit in the rest of your set. You do not have to fix it all today — but note where it appears.

Part 3 · Sequence

  1. Lay out your whole set and arrange it deliberately.
  2. Swap your first and last image and read it again. If nothing changed, the order is not doing work yet.
  3. Keep the order you can defend.

Part 4 · Critique session 4

The last one in the course. After today there is no further opportunity to evidence this outcome.

Clarity, ethical use, appropriateness — every time. If you have been quiet in the previous three sessions, this is the one to talk in.

Before you leave

  • The print exists. Not queued — printed, and looked at.
  • Both delivered files have been reopened and checked against your statements.
  • Your viewers' answers are written down verbatim, including the ones you did not want.
  • You have taken part in critique session 4.

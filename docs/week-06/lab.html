This lab is unusual: the work you do here is the submission. The consistency pass is Phase 3. There is no separate assignment task afterwards.

What you are doing

Making seven photographs into one campaign.

You have been editing on a laptop, one image at a time, for two weeks. That has made each image better and the set worse, and you have not been able to see it happening because the information was never on your screen.

Today everything goes up at once.

Part 1 · Display everything

  1. Put your entire set where you can see all of it simultaneously — the wall, a large display, or a printed contact sheet.
  2. Not a folder. Not a slideshow. All of it, at once.
  3. Stand back far enough that you cannot read detail. Detail is not what you are looking for.

Part 2 · Name the drift

  1. Write down the three things that vary most across the set.
  2. Look for: tone, colour temperature, contrast, texture and grain, and crop convention.
  3. Crop convention is the one people miss. Three tight squares and four loose landscapes is not one campaign however well the tones match.

Part 3 · Pick a target and move everything toward it

  1. Choose the image whose treatment you want the rest to match. Not necessarily the best image — the one whose handling suits the campaign.
  2. Adjust the others toward it. Work across the set, not through it one at a time.
  3. Keep everything visible while you do this. The moment you go full-screen on one image you are back to the method that caused the problem.

Part 4 · Have somebody else find the odd one out

  1. Ask a partner to look at your set with everything visible.
  2. Do not explain it first.
  3. Their job: point at the image that does not belong.
  4. If they cannot, your treatment holds. If they can, you know exactly what to fix.

Part 5 · Answer 6.3 for one image

  1. Pick one image and put it beside its unedited original.
  2. Write one sentence: what does it now say that the original did not?
  3. If you find yourself writing "it's cleaner", that is a real answer and it means the edit has not yet done what the outcome asks.

Part 6 · Assemble Phase 3

  1. Working files — layers and masks live.
  2. Exports.
  3. Your unedited originals.

Part 7 · Critique session 3

The third of four. Clarity, ethical use, appropriateness — every time, specific enough to act on.

Before you leave

  • Your whole set has been seen at once, by you and by one other person.
  • Your unedited originals are in your submission. Check this before you leave the room. They cannot be recovered later, and without them your retouching cannot be marked at all.

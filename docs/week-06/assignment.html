Week 06 Assignment — Phase 3, the campaign image set

DSGN 105 · Digital Imaging

Formative · submitted and critiqued · 0% of your course grade — and this is Phase 3, worth 25% of your campaign.

The 0% describes the weekly weight. The same submission is scored as component C on the project rubric, where it is the largest single component and the only one carrying two outcomes.

Under the formative requirement the consistency pass must be submitted before the phase it feeds — and the phase is due the same week, so those are one act.

What to submit

1 Five to seven finished campaign images
2 The working files behind them — layers and masks live, nothing flattened
3 Your unedited originals, untouched
4 One sentence per image: what it now says that its original did not
5 A disclosure statement — which images are yours, where anything else came from, how generative AI was used

Item 3 cannot be recovered later. LO 4 is assessed by comparing your edit against your original, this is the only place in the course it is assessed, and without the originals there is nothing to compare.

What a complete answer looks like

Six images from two shoots, all handled the same way: shadows held slightly open, colour pulled cool, grain retained rather than smoothed, and all cropped to the same 4:5 portrait.

One of the six, described against its original:

The original shows the volunteer behind a table with the whole room behind them — strip lights, stacked crates, three other people at the edge of frame. The edit crops to 4:5 and darkens everything past the table, so the frame is now the volunteer and the person they are handing something to. It says that this is an exchange between two people. The original said this is a busy room.

What that demonstrates: the treatment is a set of decisions applied identically rather than an average of six different corrections, the crop convention is part of it, and the sentence names what changed in the meaning rather than in the picture — checkable against an original that was actually submitted.

What does not count

  • Missing originals. Not a lost mark — a lost outcome. Nothing else in the course assesses retouching.
  • Seven excellent images with seven treatments. Individually strong and collectively unrelated is the specific failure this week exists to surface.
  • Sentences that say "it's cleaner". An honest answer, and it means the edit has not yet done what 6.1 asks.
  • Consistent tone with inconsistent crops. Crop convention is part of the treatment.
  • Flattened working files. Nothing to assess against LO 1, which component C also carries.
  • A treatment that is one preset applied to everything. Matching is not the same as a treatment carrying meaning — see the Exceeds descriptor.

Use the glossary

The terms that matter this week: retouching, treatment, consistency, unedited original, before-and-after pair. All are in the Glossary under Module 4.

Rubric

LO 4 — Editing and retouching

Edit and retouch photographs to enhance visual clarity, consistency, and communicative impact.

Level
Exceeds The treatment carries meaning across the set rather than only matching, and the student can say what each image now says that its original did not
Meets Before-and-after pairs show the edit changed what the photograph communicates, not only its cleanliness, and the treatment holds across the whole set
Approaching Each image is well retouched on its own but the treatment drifts across the set, or the edit is technically cleaner and communicates nothing new
Not yet The unedited originals are absent, so improvement cannot be judged at all

LO 1 — Raster software

Use industry-standard software to create, edit, and manipulate raster-based digital images for print and screen.

Component C carries this outcome too, on the construction of the working files. The print-and-screen half is assessed in component D, week 7.

Level
Exceeds The layer structure is legible enough that another person could pick the file up and continue it
Meets The layer structure accounts for the visible result and edits are held non-destructively
Approaching The image is well made but the file is thin
Not yet A flattened file

The same four levels apply to every assignment in this course. Full descriptors: competency rubric.
